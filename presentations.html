Pi-day: Symmetries in Mexican and Philippine Weavings

For Pi-day (March 14), students from Philippine Science High School Main Campus and Colegio de Ciencias y Humanidades Plantel Sur met to discuss similarities in Mexican and Philippine history and culture, then explored isometries and symmetries in weaving patterns. Organized by Ernesto Marquez Fragoso (FDAI 2020 Syracuse University) and Leo Andrei Crisologo (FDAI 2020 Indiana University of Pennsylvania).
